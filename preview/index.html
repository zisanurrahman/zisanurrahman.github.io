Research Focus

What I Work On

🦠

Animal Microbiome

Characterizing gut and environmental microbial communities in livestock using metagenomic and 16S rRNA approaches to improve animal health and food safety.

🤖

Machine Learning & Drug Discovery

Building ML and deep learning models trained on high-throughput chemical–genetic screens to accelerate antibiotic discovery and predict drug mechanisms of action.

✂️

CRISPR & Functional Genomics

Engineering broad-host-range CRISPRi systems for silencing gene expression in non-model bacteria, including multidrug-resistant pathogens like Burkholderia.
