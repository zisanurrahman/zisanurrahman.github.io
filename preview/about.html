I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Animal Science at the University of Manitoba, where I study the gut microbiome of livestock animals using metagenomics and computational biology approaches. My work aims to understand how microbial communities influence animal health, production efficiency, and antimicrobial resistance.

I completed my PhD in Microbiology at the Department of Microbiology, University of Manitoba, where I developed machine learning models and CRISPR-based functional genomics tools to study antibiotic resistance mechanisms and accelerate drug discovery in Burkholderia and related pathogens.

My research bridges wet-lab microbiology and data science, translating high-throughput experimental data into computational insights that inform antimicrobial stewardship and animal health management.
