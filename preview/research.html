Research

Research Interests

Applying computational and molecular tools to understand microbial communities, antibiotic resistance, and drug discovery.

Focus Areas

Current Research

🦠

Animal Microbiome & Metagenomics

Characterizing the gut, rumen, and environmental microbial communities in livestock using shotgun metagenomics and 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing.

  • Resistome and mobilome profiling in food animals
  • Microbiome–host interactions and health outcomes
  • Bioinformatics pipeline development
  • AMR gene dissemination across the food chain
🤖

Machine Learning in Drug Discovery

Developing ML and deep learning models applied to high-throughput antibacterial screening data to accelerate antibiotic discovery.

  • ML models for antibacterial screen hit rate improvement
  • Deep learning for mechanism of action prediction
  • Chemical-genetic interaction profiling
  • Active learning for compound prioritization
✂️

CRISPR & Functional Genomics

Engineering broad-host-range CRISPRi tools for silencing gene expression in non-model and multidrug-resistant bacteria.

  • CRISPRi toolkit for Burkholderia and related species
  • Essential gene identification
  • Cell envelope–antibiotic interaction mapping
  • Genetic vulnerabilities to β-lactam antibiotics
📊

Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)

Studying the epidemiology and mechanisms of AMR in livestock-associated bacteria, with a One Health perspective.

  • AMR surveillance in agricultural settings
  • Horizontal gene transfer of resistance elements
  • Stewardship interventions and microbiome effects
  • One Health approaches to AMR mitigation
