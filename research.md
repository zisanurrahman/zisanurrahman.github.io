    <h2 class="section-title">Current Research</h2>
    <div class="section-bar"></div>
    <div class="research-grid">

      <div class="research-card">
        <div class="research-card-icon">&#129440;</div>
        <h3>Animal Microbiome & Metagenomics</h3>
        <p>Characterizing the gut, rumen, and environmental microbial communities in livestock using shotgun metagenomics and 16S rRNA amplicon sequencing. Understanding how diet, management practices, and host genetics shape the microbiome.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>Resistome and mobilome profiling in food animals</li>
          <li>Microbiome–host interactions and health outcomes</li>
          <li>Bioinformatics pipeline development</li>
          <li>AMR gene dissemination across the food chain</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <div class="research-card">
        <div class="research-card-icon">&#129302;</div>
        <h3>Machine Learning in Drug Discovery</h3>
        <p>Developing ML and deep learning models applied to high-throughput antibacterial screening data. Predicting drug mechanism of action from chemical–genetic interaction profiles to identify new antibiotic candidates faster.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>ML models for antibacterial screen hit rate improvement</li>
          <li>Deep learning for mechanism of action prediction</li>
          <li>Chemical-genetic interaction profiling</li>
          <li>Active learning for compound prioritization</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <div class="research-card">
        <div class="research-card-icon">&#9986;&#65039;</div>
        <h3>CRISPR & Functional Genomics</h3>
        <p>Engineering broad-host-range CRISPRi tools for silencing gene expression in non-model bacteria. Applying these tools to interrogate essential genes and antibiotic resistance mechanisms in multidrug-resistant pathogens.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>CRISPRi toolkit for <em>Burkholderia</em> and related species</li>
          <li>Essential gene identification</li>
          <li>Cell envelope–antibiotic interaction mapping</li>
          <li>Genetic vulnerabilities to β-lactam antibiotics</li>
        </ul>
      </div>

      <div class="research-card">
        <div class="research-card-icon">&#128202;</div>
        <h3>Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR)</h3>
        <p>Studying the epidemiology and mechanisms of antimicrobial resistance in livestock-associated bacteria. Developing strategies to mitigate AMR transmission from animals to humans and the environment.</p>
        <ul>
          <li>AMR surveillance in agricultural settings</li>
          <li>Horizontal gene transfer of resistance elements</li>
          <li>Stewardship interventions and microbiome effects</li>
          <li>One Health approaches to AMR mitigation</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
